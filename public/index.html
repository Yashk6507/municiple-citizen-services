Before you submit

  • Describe one issue per complaint so it can be routed correctly.
  • Add a clear photo where it is safe to do so.
  • Place a map pin if you can, and give a landmark in the address.
  • Emergency or life-threatening situations must be reported on the emergency helpline.

A complaint is never closed automatically. It can only be marked resolved by authorised staff, with written resolution notes and optional photographic evidence.

    Contact the Municipal Corporation

    For civic complaints please use this portal so your request is recorded and tracked. The helplines below are for general enquiries.

    Head office

    Address: Municipal Corporation Head Office, Main Administrative Building
    General enquiries: 1800-000-000 (toll free)
    Complaint helpline: 1800-000-111
    Emergency services: 112
    Office hours: Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 17:30 (closed on public holidays)

    Emergency and life-threatening situations must be reported to the emergency services immediately and not through this portal.
